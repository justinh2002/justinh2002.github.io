## Profile

Justin Kin Jun Hew is a computational physics and research software practitioner whose work spans
fluid dynamics, shock interactions, magnetohydrodynamics, turbulence, numerical methods, and ice-sheet modelling software.

## Current affiliation

- **ACCESS-NRI / Australia's Climate Simulator** — Research Software Engineer, Ice Sheet Modelling team
- **Australian National University** — host institution and continuing research affiliation
- **ORCID** — [0000-0002-5238-6115](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-5238-6115)

## Education

- Bachelor of Philosophy (Science), Australian National University
- Physics major with Honours in astrophysics
- Bok Honours Scholar at ANU's Research School of Astronomy and Astrophysics

## Research domains

- Computational fluid dynamics
- Shock dynamics and shock reflection
- Magnetohydrodynamics and plasma turbulence
- Turbulent dynamos and magnetic helicity
- High-order numerical methods and summation-by-parts schemes
- Shallow-water equations
- Rarefied gas dynamics and direct simulation Monte Carlo
- Research software engineering for ice-sheet modelling
