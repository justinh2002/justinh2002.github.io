Research software engineering · computational physics

Building dependable scientific software for fluid, plasma, and climate research.

I am Justin Kin Jun Hew, a Research Software Engineer in the Ice Sheet Modelling team at ACCESS-NRI, hosted at the Australian National University. My work combines numerical methods, high-performance scientific computing, and research collaboration to support reproducible modelling workflows.

My research interests span computational fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, turbulence, shock dynamics, high-order discretisation methods, and research software for large-scale simulation projects.
